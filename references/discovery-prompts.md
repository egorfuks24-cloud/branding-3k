# Selective prompts — authored adaptation

These are not the creator's questionnaire. Ask only when a relevant answer is absent from the brief or project memory. Reuse existing answers.

- What does the customer choose or obtain, and what must be immediately recognizable?
- Who uses it, where, and what is the main action?
- Which concrete product mechanism, material, practice or history distinguishes this business? What supports that claim?
- Which feelings should the work create, and which references demonstrate them?
- What is specifically wrong with the current result: subject, sensation, business logic, usability or execution?
- Which content/name/assets are fixed, which can change, and which channels must share the identity?

If the user wants immediate progress, draft a board from known facts and label uncertain interpretations. Don't interrupt for arbitrary font/color preferences when you can prepare a concrete visual proposal.
